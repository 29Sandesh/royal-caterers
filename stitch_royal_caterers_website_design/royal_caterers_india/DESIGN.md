## Brand & Style

The visual narrative expresses modern Indian heritage hospitality through an editorial, high-end culinary lens. Rooted in the rich culinary celebrations of Madhya Pradesh, the design system balances regal poise with contemporary clarity. It communicates institutional trust, heirloom culinary expertise, and meticulous bespoke planning for grand gatherings and weddings.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on warm editorial minimalism enriched with classical tactile detailing:
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: The interface favors warm ivory over stark digital white, providing an inviting canvas evocative of fine parchment and heirloom stationery.
- **Editorial Poise**: Monumental serif headlines meet highly structured, utilitarian sans-serif body typography, referencing luxury menu curation and gourmet culinary journals.
- **Restrained Ornamentation**: Rather than ornate flourishes, the system leverages whisper-thin metallic framing, structured whitespace, and deep botanical forest greens to convey luxury without visual clutter.
- **Tone of Voice in UI**: Dignified, calm, and effortlessly knowledgeable. Information density remains measured, prioritizing culinary photography, deliberate tasting narratives, and structured service menus.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from ceremonial banquet traditions, natural foliage, and precious metal accents.

- **Primary (`#173D32`)**: A profound forest green that anchors the interface with authority, heritage, and depth. Used for dominant interactive elements, deep contrast backgrounds, primary badges, and key heading moments.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C6A56B`)**: A muted champagne gold providing subtle radiance. Reserved for key decorative dividers, active state indicators, hairline borders, verification tags, and curated call-out details. Never overused to avoid garishness.
- **Tertiary / Subdued Surface (`#E7ECE5`)**: A soft sage wash that acts as a gentle middle-tier background for item category strips, secondary chips, and quiet metadata blocks.
- **Neutral / Text Primary (`#252823`)**: Deep charcoal with a warm undertone. Replaces harsh pitch black to sustain reading comfort across editorial passages.
- **Text Secondary (`#77766F`)**: A warm limestone grey providing soft contrast for secondary metadata, catering logistics details, and dietary footnotes.
- **Background Canvas (`#FAF7F0`)**: Warm ivory foundation that eliminates glare and establishes an immediate tactile, hospitable presence.
- **Surface / Card (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, unblemished white used selectively on raised menus, tasting cards, and form containers to create quiet focus against the ivory canvas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of Playfair Display with the structural rigor of Inter.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display)**: Used strictly for narrative titles, menu chapters, hero value statements, and major pricing figures. To uphold a bespoke print heritage, heading letter-spacing is calibrated deliberately: larger headlines preserve crisp kerning, while smaller subheads (`headline-sm`, `headline-md`) embrace gentle tracking to simulate classic letterpress print.
- **Utilitarian Sans (Inter)**: Handles descriptive course narratives, interactive components, forms, and service specifications. It prevents visual fatigue across dense ingredient rosters, pricing breakdowns, and itinerary tables.
- **Ceremonial Micro-Labels**: Uppercase labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) feature increased tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`), providing a refined framing device for course tags, regional origin designations (e.g., "MALWA SPECIALTY"), and capacity indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout employs an architectural, editorial rhythm with generous negative space to evoke royal hospitality and unhurried ease.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid with a maximum content container of 1320px. 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer canvas margins of 48px (`3rem`).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
  - **Mobile (up to 767px)**: 4-column grid with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) edge margins.
- **Spatial Rhythm**:
  - Vertical section margins rely heavily on `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` to partition large editorial features, allowing photography and typography to breathe.
  - Structural cards and tasting modules utilize internal padding set to `space-lg` (desktop) and `space-md` (mobile).
  - Compact operational clusters (dietary chips, live counters, pricing tags) are locked together using tight spacing increments (`space-xs` and `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through layered surface tones and gossamer borders rather than heavy digital dropshadows, ensuring the tactile quality of premium print collateral:

- **Tiered Tonal Surfaces**: The background layer resides on Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`). Active workspaces, menu cards, and interactive forms lift onto crisp White (`#FFFFFF`). Secondary insets and filters rest comfortably on Soft Sage (`#E7ECE5`).
- **Subtle Metallic Framing**: Cards, floating panels, and distinct culinary sections utilize a 1px border colored in champagne gold with subdued opacity (`#C6A56B26` / 15% opacity), creating an unmistakable fine-dining aura.
- **Ambient Hospitality Shadows**:
  - **Resting Level**: Surfaces utilize a subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `0 2px 10px rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.04)`.
  - **Interactive Hover Level**: Cards lift gracefully with an expanded ambient diffuse: `0 12px 28px rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.08)`, accented by an increased gold border opacity (`#C6A56B4D` / 30% opacity).
  - **Modals & Overlays**: Grounded by an authoritative veil (`#173D3280`), with floating center sheets cast using `0 24px 48px rgba(37, 40, 35, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, tailored geometry (`roundedness: 1`), embodying tailored linen and fine stationery:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Applied to micro-elements such as dietary tags, status chips, checkboxes, and form inputs.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to menu cards, culinary course pods, date pickers, and interactive button containers.
- **Architectural Panels (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / 12px)**: Utilized on large hero framing cards, tasting inquiry sheets, and modal views.
- **Pill Exception**: Circular and pill radii are restricted strictly to floating live-chat controls, count indicators, and select ceremonial badging tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep forest green background (`#173D32`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` radius, with subtle 1px border (`#C6A56B40`). Hover shifts background to a slightly deepened tone (`#0F2821`) accompanied by a gentle upward transform.
- **Secondary / Champagne Outline**: Transparent background, warm charcoal text (`#252823`), bordered in solid champagne gold (`#C6A56B`). Hover fills the background with soft sage (`#E7ECE5`) and transitions text to forest green.
- **Ghost Action**: Unbordered, forest green text with persistent underline styled in champagne gold (`#C6A56B`).

### Cards & Menu Containers
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Border: 1px continuous hairline in `#C6A56B33`.
- Internal Spacing: 24px padding (`space-lg`).
- Header Treatment: Playfair Display section titles paired with an optional micro-rule separator in `#C6A56B` (height 1px, width 40px).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Soft Ivory (`#FAF7F0`).
- Border: 1px border in `#77766F40`. Focused state shifts the border cleanly to Deep Forest Green (`#173D32`) with a soft gold ring glow (`0 0 0 1px #C6A56B`).
- Typography: Inter 15px (`body-md`), label text rendered in Inter 12px uppercase (`label-md`).

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Dietary Indicators (e.g., Pure Jain, Malwi Sattvic)**: Compact 4px corner radius, soft sage background (`#E7ECE5`), forest green text (`#173D32`), with a 1px border in `#C6A56B40`.
- **Status Chips**: Muted champagne gold background with deep charcoal text for specialty catering badges.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square forms carry a 3px radius; radio buttons are circular.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#77766F`.
- Checked: Deep Forest Green fill (`#173D32`) holding a gold check glyph (`#C6A56B`).

### Lists & Menu Items
- Divided by 1px hairline rules in `#E7ECE5`.
- Culinary course items show dish titles in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`) accompanied by subtle calorie/dietary badges, with ingredient breakdowns in muted charcoal (`body-sm`).
- Pricing and serving scales align to the right in crisp Inter typography.

### Custom Catering Additions
- **Tasting Sampler Tray**: Multi-item horizontal selector card with bespoke radio selectors and live guest-count calculators.
- **Curated Menu Accordion**: Minimalist drop-panels with refined brass chevron indicators, expanding smoothly to reveal multi-course dinner allocations.
